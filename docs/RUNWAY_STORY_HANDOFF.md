# Runway 30-second story: operational notes

- Preview branch `feat/production-gates-whatsapp` adds migration `015_runway_story.sql`. Apply through the existing admin migration route before testing in Preview. Production is not promoted by this change.
- Runway is the default selection for *new* video production runs; Faceless can still be selected. Existing runs, including failed paid Faceless runs, retain their original provider and cannot be silently reset, so a new job is needed for a new provider.
- The saved script must be explicitly approved via WhatsApp and have exactly 30 seconds duration. The Runway path calls `wan3` at 720p, 30 seconds, with audio. The current published Runway rate is 10 credits/second: expected 300 credits ($3 before taxes). Runway organization balance is retrieved live for the quote and checked again immediately before the single paid start. Any insufficient balance blocks approval/start.
- Provide a permitted HTTPS image URL and explicitly confirm the rights to transform and upload it. Direct Amazon image URLs and Amazon CDN hosts are rejected. The Amazon product URL and ASIN serve identification only. Amazon PartnerNet's limited license does not authorize downloading images, giving them to a third-party generative renderer, or retaining derivative outputs. Seller-granted image rights or a self-owned photograph are needed for a model-faithful visual. An independent illustrative image can represent the product category but must never be passed off as the exact product.
- WhatsApp render approval includes the product, ASIN, image URL, model, duration, estimated credits, current balance, USD estimate before tax and the storyboard. Provider writes are claimed in Postgres before dispatch; ambiguous responses must be reconciled manually without another paid POST.
- Runway's temporary MP4 is copied into the public Blob `reels/` path. A separately approved Instagram publication is still required. The generated speech, use of product, visual fidelity and correct action require human preview before publication; a prompt does not guarantee exact voiceover.
- Forty seconds need at least a 30-second task plus another 10-second task and a compositing step. WAN 3 at 720p would be at least 400 credits ($4 before tax), plus potential editing/voice charges. This branch intentionally does not offer a 40-second start without a verified stitching and cost gate.
- Check that Vercel Preview has `RUNWAYML_API_SECRET`, `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`, WhatsApp settings and the migration. No provider POST is performed by migration, build or tests.
